Our Story

Empowering Students to Study Anywhere

knowYourUniversity is a global education directory built by former international students. We believe every student deserves clarity on where and how to study abroad — no gatekeeping, no confusion.

Mission

Every student, a global citizen.

We build tools that help students find the right course, connect with universities, and unlock scholarships — regardless of background or budget.

Vision

A world where geography never limits a student's education potential.

Values

Transparency, accessibility and student-first thinking guide every product decision.

Community

Alumni mentors from 40+ countries actively support prospective students on our platform.

What We Do

Six services, one goal — get you into the right university.

Every service is offered à la carte or as a bundled package. Free first consultation, always.

Course & College Matching

Personality × budget × goal → shortlist of 5–10 perfect-fit universities.

1:1 Consultant Support

Ex-alumni experts dedicated to your specific target region.

SOP / Essay / LOR

Multi-round drafting with your consultant + native-speaker editing.

Scholarship Applications

Curate + apply to 5–8 scholarships aligned with your profile.

Visa & Pre-Departure

Mock interviews, document checklists, forex, and travel prep.

Housing & On-arrival

Curated student housing, banking setup and campus onboarding.
